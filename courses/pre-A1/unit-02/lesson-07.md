# ✏️ 10. Małe obliczenia

Oblicz wynik i zapisz go **po angielsku**.

### 1.

10 + 2 = ___ → __________________

### 2.

10 + 5 = ___ → __________________

### 3.

20 - 3 = ___ → __________________

### 4.

9 + 9 = ___ → __________________

### 5.

16 - 3 = ___ → __________________

### 6.

20 - 1 = ___ → __________________

### ✅ Sprawdź

1. 10 + 2 = **12 → twelve**
2. 10 + 5 = **15 → fifteen**
3. 20 - 3 = **17 → seventeen**
4. 9 + 9 = **18 → eighteen**
5. 16 - 3 = **13 → thirteen**
6. 20 - 1 = **19 → nineteen**
