# 📚 4. One or more?

Zapisz liczbę mnogą.

1. book → __________
2. phone → __________
3. box → __________
4. chair → __________
5. bus → __________
6. baby → __________
7. key → __________
8. watch → __________
9. city → __________
10. cup → __________

### ✅ Sprawdź

1. **books**
2. **phones**
3. **boxes**
4. **chairs**
5. **buses**
6. **babies**
7. **keys**
8. **watches**
9. **cities**
10. **cups**

### Remember

Najczęściej dodajemy **-s**:

**book → books**

Czasami dodajemy **-es**:

**box → boxes**

W niektórych słowach zmieniamy **-y** na **-ies**:

**city → cities**

Ale:

**key → keys**

# 💬 12. A short conversation

Uzupełnij rozmowę.

Wykorzystaj informacje:

**Object 1:** book, red, near

**Object 2:** phone, black, far

**Object 3:** umbrella, blue, near

### Conversation

**A:** What's __________?

**B:** It's __________ red book.

**A:** What colour is it?

**B:** It's __________.

**A:** And what's __________?

**B:** It's __________ black phone.

**A:** What colour is it?

**B:** It's __________.

**A:** What's this?

**B:** It's __________ blue umbrella.

### ✅ Sprawdź

**A:** What's **this**?

**B:** It's **a** red book.

**A:** What colour is it?

**B:** It's **red**.

**A:** And what's **that**?

**B:** It's **a** black phone.

**A:** What colour is it?

**B:** It's **black**.

**A:** What's this?

**B:** It's **a** blue umbrella.
