# 10. The Newspaper Vendor

Smith describes a small interaction between her friend Jonathan and a newspaper vendor.

Jonathan does not have the correct change.

The vendor offers him the newspaper anyway.

Jonathan goes elsewhere to get change so that he can pay.

Instead of appreciating this, the vendor feels hurt.

Discuss.

1. Why was the vendor hurt even though Jonathan's intention was reasonable?
2. What does the example suggest about the difference between a **transaction** and a **human interaction**?
3. Do very small interactions genuinely contribute to belonging?
4. Can efficiency sometimes undermine human connection?
5. Where does this happen in modern life?

Think about:

- self-service technology
- remote work
- online shopping
- social media
- public transport
- customer service
