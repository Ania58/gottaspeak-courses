# 4. Language From the Listening

Work with these expressions.

- instant gratification – preference for immediate pleasure over greater long-term rewards
- deadline-driven behaviour – tendency to become productive only when time pressure becomes unavoidable
- cognitive avoidance – unconscious tendency to avoid tasks that generate discomfort or anxiety
- self-imposed pressure – psychological stress created by one's own expectations or standards
- long-term procrastination – repeated postponement of important life goals that lack immediate deadlines
- behavioural momentum – increasing tendency to continue an activity once it has been successfully started
- anticipatory anxiety – emotional discomfort experienced before beginning a demanding task
- self-defeating behaviour – actions that unintentionally undermine one's own goals or success
- intrinsic motivation – desire to perform an activity because it is personally meaningful rather than externally rewarded
- perceived urgency – subjective feeling that a task has become impossible to ignore
