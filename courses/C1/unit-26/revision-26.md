# 4. Language From the Listening

Work with these expressions.

- persuasive delivery – manner of speaking that effectively influences an audience through clarity, confidence and engagement
- vocal presence – ability to command attention through one's voice rather than authority or status
- conversational credibility – degree to which a speaker is perceived as trustworthy, authentic and reliable
- rhetorical authenticity – communicating in a manner that genuinely reflects one's beliefs and values
- vocal modulation – deliberate variation of pitch, pace, volume and tone to enhance meaning and maintain attention
- communicative integrity – consistency between what a person says and how they behave
- audience engagement – active psychological involvement of listeners throughout communication
- reflective silence – intentional pause allowing listeners to process information before continuing
- vocal resonance – richness and depth of the voice that contributes to authority and warmth
- conscious listening – deliberate effort to fully understand a speaker before forming a response
